Community Building

Creating a supportive, collaborative, and inclusive network for women in computational biology.

Shared Purpose

Community building provides a social and scientific foundation where researchers can connect, exchange ideas, and find support at all career stages.

Our activities foster belonging, confidence, and long-term collaboration.

Peer Support Spaces

We organize spaces where participants can discuss challenges openly, share practical advice, and identify collective solutions for scientific and professional growth.

Sustainable Collaboration

By strengthening relationships across groups and disciplines, the community generates opportunities for mentorship, training, co-authorship, and project development.
