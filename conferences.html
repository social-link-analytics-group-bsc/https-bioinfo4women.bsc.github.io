Conferences

Connecting communities, sharing advances, and promoting women-led bioinformatics research at global and local events.

Why Conferences Matter

Conferences are strategic spaces to showcase scientific work, build collaborations, and increase visibility for women researchers in computational biology and AI for health.

Our programme supports participation, encourages inclusive panels, and creates opportunities for early-career researchers to present their work.

Community and Exchange

Beyond presentations, conference activities include mentoring moments, networking sessions, and practical conversations about career pathways and leadership in science.

These exchanges help create an active and supportive research ecosystem.

Impact and Visibility

By participating in conferences, we amplify research on sex and gender dimensions in science and contribute to better standards for responsible, inclusive computational research.
