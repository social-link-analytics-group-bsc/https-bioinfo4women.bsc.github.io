Fellows Programme

Women scientists who advanced from BSC postdoctoral research to principal investigator leadership roles.

Programme Overview

A Fellow to the Bioinfo4Women programme is a woman scientist who, after completing postdoctoral research at the Barcelona Supercomputing Center, has moved into leadership as a principal investigator in another research institution. As PIs, these scientists lead projects, mentor junior researchers, and drive discoveries in their fields.

These fellows are role models for aspiring scientists: they combine scientific excellence with leadership, and contribute to breaking down gender barriers in academia and research.

Vera Pancaldi

Group Leader at the Centre de Recherches en Cancérologie de Toulouse and Senior Researcher at BSC. She joined the Computational Genomics group in 2017 after prior research stages at CNIO, University of Cambridge, the Sanger Institute, and UCL.

Her research focuses on computational methods for gene regulation and epigenomics, with special interest in understanding how gene-expression changes contribute to disease.

Anaïs Baudot

CNRS researcher and professor in Computational Biology, leading the Networks and Systems Biology for Diseases group in Marseille, with affiliation to BSC as an Established Researcher. Her interests include AI and digital approaches for human genetic diseases, especially rare diseases.

Her work has contributed to understanding biological network structures in disease contexts, and to developing computational tools for high-throughput genomic data analysis.

Milana Frenkel-Morgenster

Computational biologist at Bar-Ilan University (Israel), heading the Genomic Data Science Lab and affiliated with the Azrieli Faculty of Medicine. Her research addresses genomic data analysis and applications to personalized medicine.

Her lab has developed computational tools to study genomic variation and disease, including methods to predict variant impact on protein structure and function, with broad contribution to international bioinformatics research.
