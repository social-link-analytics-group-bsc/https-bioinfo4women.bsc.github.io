Fellows Programme

Supporting promising researchers with training, mentorship, and visibility.

Programme Overview

The fellows programme is designed to empower women researchers at key moments of their scientific careers. It combines mentorship, peer exchange, and tailored support for independent research development.

Participants receive practical guidance to translate their ideas into robust research plans.

Peer Learning and Community

Participants engage in peer-learning sessions where they exchange practical experience and build a long-term support network.

Leadership Visibility

Fellows are encouraged to present their work in seminars and events, increasing visibility and facilitating collaboration opportunities.
