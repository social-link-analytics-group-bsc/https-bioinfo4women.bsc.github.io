BSC Life Sciences Open Day

An annual Bioinfo4Women initiative where university students explore current advances in biomedicine, meet women researchers in bioinformatics, and discover computational research at BSC.

What Is Open Day?

The Barcelona Supercomputing Center hosts this annual event for university students interested in bioinformatics. As part of Bioinfo4Women and around the International Day of Women and Girls in Science, the programme offers an accessible introduction to research, technology, and real career paths in computational biology.

Participants engage directly with researchers through informal talks on personalized medicine, genome analysis, NLP applied to biomedicine, genome evolution, and scientific infrastructure.

Programme Highlights

One of the most appreciated parts of Open Day is the opportunity to connect with women researchers in a friendly and informal environment. Students can ask practical questions and see how computational methods are used in real biomedical projects.

The experience also includes a virtual visit to MareNostrum 4, Spain’s most powerful supercomputer, helping attendees understand how high-performance computing supports cutting-edge research.

Editions

  • 2023 Held in person with high participation and strong attendee feedback.
  • 2022 Organized online due to pandemic context.
  • 2021 First online edition under COVID restrictions.
  • 2020 Reduced participants due to exceptional conditions.
  • 2019 First edition of the B4W Life Sciences Open Day.

Participant Experience

Places are limited, which keeps discussions close and interactive. Feedback has been notably positive, with participants highlighting both scientific content and the welcoming atmosphere.

“I think it was a very grateful experience and I have learned a lot. I am looking forward to coming back to BSC!”
