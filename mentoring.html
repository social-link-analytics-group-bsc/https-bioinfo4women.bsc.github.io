Mentoring Programme

Support for career growth, scientific leadership, and transition to independence.

Programme Overview

Our mentoring programme pairs early-career and mid-career researchers with experienced mentors in computational biology. The goal is to support professional development through practical guidance, personalized feedback, and sustained community support.

Each cycle includes clear objectives, periodic follow-up, and tailored support based on each participant’s career stage and scientific focus.

Regular Mentor-Mentee Follow-up

Structured follow-up meetings provide a safe space to discuss challenges, opportunities, and concrete action plans throughout the year.

Participants define personal goals such as leadership progression, publication strategy, and transition planning to independent positions.

Skills and Leadership Development

The programme includes practical sessions on grant writing, scientific communication, collaboration building, and team management.
