Networking Programme

Connecting women in computational biology through targeted sessions, workshops, and leadership-focused activities.

Connect Session: Women in Computational Biology (ECCB 2020)

The Network & Connect Session on Women in Computational Biology was held on September 7, 2020, during ECCB2020. The session created space for women in computational biology to share knowledge, experiences, and proposals to strengthen women’s role in the field.

The event included five 10-minute presentations by different associations, followed by a 35-minute Q&A discussion.

Workshop at Palau Macaya (2021)

The networking workshop on Gender Bias in Artificial Intelligence and Health focused on building a future for equality through collaboration among organizations working on gender equity.

Participants identified concrete needs: more women in AI postdoctoral programmes, more funding opportunities, more women editing Wikipedia, and stronger resources to increase impact in women-focused initiatives.

Meeting with Women Leaders

Within the inauguration of the BSC-Repsol building, B4W organized a networking activity (October 8) to connect BSC staff—especially women researchers and technicians—with women leaders from different sectors.

The goal was to challenge perceptions about women leadership, learn from real career experiences, strengthen internal community, and collect concerns around career progression and equity-related issues.
